Preferences pane

Theme, font size, density and motion — personal, per device, applied instantly. Replaces the “Select Theme” dialog.

The pane opened on the right is live. Change the theme, drag the font-size slider, switch density — every mockup remembers your choice (localStorage). Close it and reopen from the gear in the rail footer, the user menu, or ⌘,.

Why a pane, not a page

Preferences are glanceable, low-stakes and personal. A right drawer keeps the current screen in view so the effect of a theme or font-size change is visible immediately.

Production notes
  • New PreferencesProvider owns the five attributes; keep the old app-theme/theme keys as read-through aliases for one release.
  • Theme grid = radiogroup; each card is a button with the swatch preview; applies on click (no Save).
  • Font-size slider updates <html> font-size; audit hard-coded px in components → tokens.
  • Radix Themes scaling follows the font scale; accentColor ← --accent.
