Scope precedence & resolution order

How a property’s type lookup resolves, most specific first.

  1. 1Tenant namespace (tenant/<slug>/…)Most specific — the tenant’s own private types win.
  2. 2Imported vendor namespaces (vendor/…)Vendor schemas imported for that tenant.
  3. 3System core (std/…)Shared fallback available to every tenant.
Core types may be referenced by tenant types; tenant types are never visible across tenants or from core.
